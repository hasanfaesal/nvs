## Goal
Two good phone videos, two takes each, of static indoor scenes:
- **`desk_01`:** a tabletop with 8–12 distinct objects;
- **`shelf_01`:** a cluttered desk or shelf with repeated categories and partial occlusion.

## Read first
1. `docs/spec/07-phase-a.md` §8 (the capture protocol, followed exactly)

## Steps
1. **Stage `desk_01`:**
   - 8–12 objects with **distinct, nameable** identities (e.g. red mug, stapler, rubber duck, blue notebook, headphones, apple, glasses case, toy car);
   - a textured tablecloth or newspaper underneath;
   - matte items preferred.
2. **Stage `shelf_01`:** 10–12 objects including **repeated categories** (2 mugs, 3 books, 2 pens), with some partial occlusion.
3. **Phone settings:** AE/AF lock; HDR, night mode and beauty off; 1080p or 4K at 30 fps; landscape.
4. **Record:** a slow orbit, 2 loops at different heights, 60–90 s, **two takes per scene**. Check playback for blur and exposure jumps right away.
5. **Write down the object list** (the exact names you'll use as labels, lower-case, e.g. `red mug`) in Findings. The same names must be used in T-C03.
6. **Copy the videos to the lab PC:** `~/nvs/data/raw/custom/desk_01/video.mp4` and `…/shelf_01/video.mp4` (keep the second takes as `video_b.mp4`).
